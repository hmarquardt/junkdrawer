Sources & Caveats

  • Gibson County GIS/Mapping: parcels, roads, addresses, boundary.
  • Indiana DNR: Best Available Floodplain county downloads.
  • USFWS NWI: Wetlands REST services.
  • IndianaMap / IN GIS: Statewide roads and streams.
  • OpenStreetMap: Basemap.
  • iNaturalist: Citizen-science observations.
  • GBIF: Biodiversity occurrence records.
  • eBird: Cornell Lab of Ornithology bird sightings.
  • Indiana Herp Atlas / HerpMapper: Herp records (manual import).

This app is for planning and visualization only. Not legal, property, or ecological advice. Respect private property, access laws, and protected species. Do not trespass to reach obscured coordinates.
