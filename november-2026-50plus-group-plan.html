Good people.
Better conversations.

A month built around the formats that have been working: music and memory, food, pets, everyday personality, open-feed breathing room — plus two new showcase days, Thanksgiving, and a perfectly shameless Black Friday consumerism whiplash.

21 core posts 4 feature days 4 open days 1 optional 30 days planned

November operating idea

Keep the successful rhythm recognizable without making it feel programmed. Mondays remain Memory/Music, Fridays remain Foodie Friday, Wednesdays mostly stay out of the way, and Saturdays become the month’s showcase lane. Thanksgiving week shifts from anticipation → gratitude → Black Friday absurdity → leftovers → decompression.

Showcase peopleTalent and Side Hustle days let members contribute something they care about instead of merely answering another question.
Protect organic threadsAn OPEN day is real. If members are already carrying the feed, admins do not need to manufacture another conversation.
Holiday without overloadThanksgiving appears throughout the final week, but the entire month is not one long gratitude exercise.
Contrast is funThanksgiving’s sincere post is intentionally followed by a Black Friday consumerism confessional the next morning.

Two new tentpole posts

🎤 Nov. 7 — Show Us Your Talent

Make it visual. Encourage photos and short videos. This works best if admins seed the thread with their own examples so members understand that “talent” includes practical, artistic, musical, mechanical, culinary and weirdly specific skills.

💼 Nov. 14 — Show Us Your Side Hustle

Make this a controlled promotion thread rather than letting sales posts leak across the feed. One link per person, no unsolicited DMs, and no recruiting-pressure business models. The thread becomes genuinely useful instead of spammy.
