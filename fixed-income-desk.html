Data methodology & provenance
Treasury nominal and real par curves are daily official estimates using indicative market quotations. They are not zero curves, streaming data or executable prices. NY Fed rates are published reference rates, not tradeable offers. These sources are fetched directly; no proxy or backend is used.
Comparisons choose the latest observation on or before a calendar target (up to 10 calendar days earlier), never a future observation. “Previous” selects the preceding observation. Calendar month/year shifts clamp to month end. The actual selected date is always shown. Dates remain distinct across sources; 5Y/10Y breakevens use the latest common nominal/real date. No forward-fill is used in historical breakevens.
Yield differences × 100 give basis points. Level is the equal-weight mean of 2Y/5Y/10Y/30Y when all exist. Front slope = 2Y−3M; belly slope = 10Y−2Y; long slope = 30Y−10Y. Butterfly = 2×5Y−2Y−10Y, in bp. Mean change across matched tenors approximates a parallel shift; RMS residual reports nonparallel movement. Steepening/flattening describes the change in 2s10s, not an economic forecast.
Freshness uses source observation dates, never the retrieval date. Weekends and US federal holidays are excluded from an approximate publication-lag counter; more than two business days is flagged stale. This is a display heuristic, not a guarantee of a complete calendar or today's publication. Refresh failures preserve available session data. Longer history is fetched only when requested, capped at five years; market data and AI responses are never persisted.
All analytics are local deterministic JavaScript. AI commentary is interpretation, not a market-data source. Nominal/real par-yield differences are approximate inflation compensation and are affected by curve construction, liquidity and risk premia. Revisit breakeven limitations →

Conventions to carry into any real calculation
  • Identify clean/dirty price, face scale, currency, settlement date, day count and compounding before comparing numbers.
  • Specify a curve and bump for duration/PV01; dollar-duration scaling and signed risk conventions vary.
  • BEY, eligible YTW calls, I-spread benchmarks, asset-swap packages and discount margin have vendor or contract variations.
  • OAS, effective risk, prepayment and implied default probabilities are model-dependent.
  • Labs omit actual calendars, fees, taxes, default and embedded options. Inputs are illustrative scenarios, not current market observations.
