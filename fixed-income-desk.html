Frank J. Fabozzi’s The Handbook of Fixed Income Securities supplies the organizing tradition: valuation, yield curves, risk, sectors and portfolio practice. This is an original refresher, not an excerpt, chapter-by-chapter digest or claim to reproduce a specific edition. The Handbook was not used as a source of quoted text.

Equations are standard mathematical relationships, with independently calculated illustrative examples. Modern factual claims are grounded in the sources below; qualitative risk rankings and workflow comparisons are author synthesis. Vendor sources establish available capabilities, not independently verified performance.

Conventions to carry into any real calculation

  • Identify clean/dirty price, face scale, currency, settlement date, day count and compounding before comparing numbers.
  • Specify a curve and bump for duration/PV01; dollar-duration scaling and signed risk conventions vary.
  • BEY, eligible YTW calls, I-spread benchmarks, asset-swap packages and discount margin have vendor or contract variations.
  • OAS, effective risk, prepayment and implied default probabilities are model-dependent.
  • Labs omit actual calendars, fees, taxes, default and embedded options. Inputs are illustrative scenarios, not current market observations.
