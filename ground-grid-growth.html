Ground, Grid and Growth

What Southern Indiana should know before saying yes—or no—to a data center. Follow the land, the farm business, the utility contract, and the public deal.

This project does not ask you to support data centers. It asks you to understand the land, farming economy, infrastructure, and public deal well enough to oppose or support a specific proposal for defensible reasons.

What this project is not

Not a campaign for data centers.Not an anti-development site. Not individualized legal, tax, or investment advice.Not a substitute for project-specific utility and planning documents.
