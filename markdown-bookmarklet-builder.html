💡 How It Works

  1. Click the bookmarklet while reading a page you want to save.
  2. It scans the document for the most readable content container.
  3. It converts headings, paragraphs, links, lists, blockquotes, code, and tables into clean Markdown.
  4. It copies the result directly to your clipboard.
  5. A small card slides in to confirm the copy, then vanishes.

The Art of the Pocket Tool

A well-made pocket tool is judged not by the number of blades, but by the clarity of its purpose. Each element must earn its place.

"Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication."

When building utilities for the web, we should aim for the same restraint:

  • No external dependencies
  • Self-contained logic
  • Graceful failure modes

Known Limitations

    
    
      
  • Bookmarklets may not run on browser internal pages (chrome://, file://, etc.).
    • 
      
  • Some websites with strict Content Security Policy block inline scripts.
    • 
      
  • Clipboard write may require HTTPS or a recent user interaction.
    • 
      
  • Dynamic single-page apps may produce messy or incomplete output.
    • 
      
  • Paywalled or private content is not bypassed; only visible DOM is converted.
    • 
      
  • This tool only converts what is present in the DOM at the moment you click.
    • 
    


    
Security & Privacy

    

      Privacy note: The bookmarklet runs only when you click it. It does not send page content anywhere. It copies Markdown to your local clipboard. It cannot bypass site restrictions, logins, or paywalls. Some pages may block bookmarklets or clipboard access.
