The point of all this
Make it easy to join in, interesting enough to tell a story, and loose enough that the group still feels like people talking to people — not admins filling boxes on a calendar.
October guardrails: no politics, no profile-rating games, no dating grievance bait, no pressure to post photos, and no need to use AI if you’d rather just watch the chaos. The schedule is a spark, not a rulebook.
