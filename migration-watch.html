📖 About / Getting Started

What is Migration Watch?

Migration Watch helps you see what birds are moving through your area right now. It combines recent bird sightings with seasonal patterns to help answer questions such as: are hummingbirds moving through right now, are Sandhill Cranes getting closer, has activity picked up since yesterday, and is today worth making a birding trip? You do not need to be an experienced birder to use it.

Get started

  1. Paste your free eBird key into Settings. Migration Watch uses eBird for recent sightings — think of the key as a pass that lets this page ask eBird for recent sightings. eBird requires each person using its data service to have a free personal key. Get one at ebird.org/api/keygen.
  2. Choose your location — press ◎ Locate Me or search for any city or place.
  3. Refresh the sightings — Migration Watch will gather recent reports near that location.
  4. Look at the Go Looking Score — the dashboard summarizes whether activity looks quiet, possible, good, very good, or strong (Quiet 0–24, Possible 25–44, Good 45–64, Very Good 65–79, Go Now 80–100). It is a decision aid, not a guarantee that you will see a bird.

Why do I need an eBird key?

eBird provides the recent bird sightings used by Migration Watch. eBird requires each person using its data service to have a free personal key — think of the key as a pass that lets this page ask eBird for recent sightings. Your account and personal key are both free, your key is stored only in your browser, and Migration Watch does not ship with any key. Migration Watch connects directly from your browser to eBird; your key is sent to eBird only when bird sightings are requested.

Optional: AI explanation

Migration Watch does not need AI to find birds, calculate scores, detect trends, or decide whether activity is increasing — those parts are calculated directly from the bird data. If you add an OpenRouter key, you can optionally ask an AI model to turn the numbers into a more conversational explanation. OpenRouter gives Migration Watch access to many different AI models; after adding a key, choose the model you want in Settings. Different models may have different prices, speeds, and capabilities. Without OpenRouter you still get live sightings, map, featured species, movement history, unusual sightings, watchlist, and deterministic signals; with OpenRouter you additionally get optional plain-English AI interpretation. AI is still completely optional. Get a key at openrouter.ai/keys (OpenRouter usage may have a cost depending on the model used).

Your location

Migration Watch needs a starting point so it knows which sightings count as nearby. You can press ◎ Locate Me, search a place, or use a preset. Locate Me is a one-time location check — Migration Watch does not continuously track your movement.

How does it know migration is changing?

Migration Watch saves a tiny daily summary of the numbers it calculated — not a copy of all the bird sightings. That lets it notice changes such as hummingbird reports surging or the nearest crane reports getting closer. Summaries are stored only in your browser, history is kept 90 days, and you can clear it from Settings.

What is actually observed?

OBSERVED = a real recent bird report from a source such as eBird. MODELED = an estimate based on seasonal timing. INFERRED = a conclusion calculated from multiple signals — for example, Sandhill Cranes moving closer is an inference based on the changing distance of recent reports; it does not mean Migration Watch is tracking a particular flock.

Where Should I Go?

Migration Watch can compare nearby birding locations using today's recent sightings, migration trends, distance and the kind of outing you want. Recommendations are estimates based on recent reports and published site information — public access should always be verified before visiting, and bird sightings are never guaranteed. Some recommended locations come from eBird reporting activity alone; those may not have verified parking or access details.

Hummingbirds

Migration Watch tracks Ruby-throated Hummingbirds, the primary eastern species, using recent nearby observations plus seasonal timing to indicate whether movement appears early, building, peak, declining, late, or done. Keeping feeders up during migration does not prevent hummingbirds from migrating.

Sandhill Cranes

For crane users the card looks for recent reports, flock sizes, proximity, whether reports are moving closer, and migration timing. For Indiana-area users Jasper-Pulaski may be shown as supplemental regional context when reliable data are available; it does not represent your local crane count.

Where does the information come from?

eBird (primary live sighting source; credit Cornell Lab of Ornithology / eBird), OpenStreetMap (map background; credit OpenStreetMap contributors), and Migration Watch (trend calculations, movement signals, seasonal logic, Go Looking Score). These are calculations performed by this page, not official eBird ratings. BirdCast, Journey North, and Indiana DNR are potential supplemental sources; Migration Watch does not maintain a verified live connection to them today.

Privacy

  • Your eBird and OpenRouter keys are saved only in this browser.
  • Migration Watch does not continuously track your location.
  • Raw bird sightings are not permanently saved — only small daily trend summaries are kept locally, and you can clear migration history from Settings.
  • Migration Watch normally connects directly from your browser to eBird. Your eBird key is sent to eBird only when Migration Watch requests bird sightings. The key is stored locally in this browser and is not included in migration history, exports, Analytics, or OpenRouter requests.
  • OpenRouter receives data only when you explicitly request the optional AI interpretation.

Technical details ▸ static GitHub Pages application · Migration Watch connects directly from the browser to the eBird API (an opt-in web relay exists only as a developer fallback) · no Migration Watch application server exists · raw eBird responses are memory-only · derived daily snapshots are local · AI is optional.
