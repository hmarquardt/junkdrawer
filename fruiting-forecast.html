Non-negotiable field rule
Finding is not identifying
This app never determines that a mushroom is safe to eat.
Opportunity scores estimate the likelihood of fruiting conditions for a broad taxon and area. They do not establish identity, edibility, freshness, contamination, land access, or collecting legality. Species groups can include toxic or poorly known members.
Use multiple current field references, inspect all diagnostic features, make a spore print where appropriate, and obtain in-person verification from a qualified local expert before consumption. When in doubt, leave it.
