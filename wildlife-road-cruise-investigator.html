    How it decides

    You provide only a place and a radius. The Investigator discovers candidate landscapes, rejects the unsuitable ones, investigates the actual roads, verifies access and wildlife evidence, pulls route geometry from mapped road data, and ranks what survives. It only asks you a question when the geography itself is ambiguous.

    Agentic web search runs through OpenRouter; road geometry comes from OpenStreetMap via targeted, rate-limited queries — never invented. The exported JSON is the portable record of everything found and everything still uncertain.

      Package structure (schemaVersion 1)

      guide — title, resolved location, radius, philosophy. routes[] — the published field-guide opportunities: content, access, scores, GeoJSON geometry, evidence-tiered wildlife. research — provenance: pipeline stage records, candidate trail (kept vs rejected), road findings, deduplicated sources, warnings, unresolved questions, usage counters. fieldReports[] — reserved for future human observations; automated research never writes here.

      Research evidence (facts) is stored separately from interpretation. Species entries carry an evidence tier; anything marked habitat-supported or seasonal-possibility is inference, not record.
