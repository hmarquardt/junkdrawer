Wildlife Field Recorder

Offline-first field recorder for wildlife sightings, road cruising, birding, herping, and general nature observation.

How this works
🎙 Voice / 🖼 Import Photo → Local Queue → Process Audio → Classify Subject → Create/Update Trip → Submit to Backend → Attach Photos → Review/Map
Features
  • Quick voice notes with GPS and timestamp
  • Import a photo → metadata or outing-track time/place recovery → weather → AI identification
  • Offline-first: everything saved locally
  • Weather snapshot when online
  • LLM transcription and classification
  • Auto-group observations into trips/routes
  • Submit enriched records + audio to LabBox
  • Attach photos to submitted observations
  • Review trips on a map with route lines

Useful for herping, birding, road cruising, wildlife photography, and general naturalist notes.
