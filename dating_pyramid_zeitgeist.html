What is this?

This page is a playful cultural radar for dating discourse. It does not claim to be science, prophecy, therapy, or a substitute for touching grass. It watches the language floating around dating apps, surveys, advice articles, Reddit threads, and user-added sources, then turns the recurring themes into two pyramids: one for what people seem to want, and one for what makes them want to fake their own death and move to a new zip code.

It blends app reports, surveys, news/lifestyle sources, Reddit/social chatter, and optional manually pasted material. Reddit and social sources are intentionally weighted equally by default with research and app reports, because lived experience often shows up there before institutions summarize it.

The pyramid has five tiers. In Romance mode, they climb from Attraction up to Romantic Self-Actualization. In Red Flags mode, they climb from Petty Icks up to Run Level. The words inside each tier are sized by how loudly the current dating discourse is talking about them.

OpenRouter is optional. Without it, the page still builds the pyramids using local rules and seeded data. With it, the page can generate a short plain-English readout of what the current terms suggest about the dating mood. If you want to try it, grab a key and learn more here:

How the scoring works

The page extracts phrases from enabled sources, scores them by frequency and source weight, then boosts or filters them based on your selected lens (General, Over 50, Gen Z, Women's discourse, Men's discourse). AI is not used for baseline classification — it is only an optional enhancement for the "Explain this pyramid" feature.

Privacy

Everything stays in your browser. Sources, cache, and OpenRouter settings are stored in localStorage only. No external server is involved except the sources you choose to fetch and the optional OpenRouter call.
